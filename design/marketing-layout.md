# Marketing Layout

Layout guidance for processpilot.com, implemented in Phase 2. Page list
and content per section are defined in
[product/information-architecture.md](../product/information-architecture.md);
this document covers layout structure and tone, not page-by-page copy.

## Shell structure

- **Header** — logo (once supplied, see [branding.md — asset status](branding.md)),
  primary nav (Product, Solutions, Industries, Pricing, Security,
  Resources, Company), Sign in, and a single primary CTA (Start trial or
  Request demo — not both competing for primary emphasis on every page).
- **Footer** — secondary navigation, legal links (Privacy, Terms),
  company/contact information.
- **Page sections** — generous spacing per
  [spacing.md — marketing section padding](spacing.md), in contrast to
  the denser application layout.

## Page composition pattern

Most marketing pages (Product, Solutions, Industries) follow: hero
(value proposition specific to that page's audience) → supporting detail
sections (feature depth, tied back to the
[product loop](../product/vision.md) stages where relevant) → a single
closing CTA section. Avoid stacking multiple competing CTAs down a single
page.

## Content integrity rules

Per [product/product-principles.md](../product/product-principles.md)
and the [avoid list](branding.md):

- No fabricated customer logos, testimonials, certifications, or success
  statistics anywhere on the marketing site.
- Security/compliance claims on the Security page must reflect actual,
  current posture (see [docs/architecture](../docs/architecture/) and
  [product/assumptions-and-risks.md](../product/assumptions-and-risks.md))
  — not aspirational claims about future phases stated as present fact.
- Pricing shown publicly must be committed pricing, not the working
  hypotheses in [product/pricing-hypotheses.md](../product/pricing-hypotheses.md).
