# Customer Complaint Process

Status: **Draft.**

## How a complaint is received

Via support@processpilot.com, privacy@processpilot.com, or
legal@processpilot.com depending on subject matter (see the Privacy
Policy and Terms of Service for these contact points).

## Handling

1. Acknowledge receipt within 2 business days.
2. Investigate using the audit trail where the complaint concerns product
   behavior or data handling.
3. Respond with findings and, where applicable, remediation, within 10
   business days — longer for complex investigations, with an interim
   update at the 10-day mark.
4. Complaints alleging a legal or regulatory violation are escalated to
   whoever holds legal responsibility (see
   [ownership-matrix.md](ownership-matrix.md)) rather than resolved
   informally by support.
